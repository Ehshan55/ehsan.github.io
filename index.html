About me

I’m Md Ehsan, a senior software engineer based in West Bengal, India. At Scrollengine I lead frontend and support engineering for a Shopify app that handles local delivery and store pickup, and I answer merchants’ questions before they sign up.

For freelance clients I handle the whole journey: I learn how your business runs, recommend what fits, build or set it up properly and stay around after launch.
